The Studio

We make games about becoming.

Orexis is a solo indie studio building games where the act of combination is the heart of play. Not merging as a UI mechanic, but as a philosophy. Two things meet. Something new emerges that neither could have been alone.

Every game we make is built around that moment. The instant of transformation. The thing on the other side of it.
